# One-Line Office Jokes — candidate tournament

Bounty: `u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc` — $2 USDC, 5 places
(1st $0.73 / 2nd $0.50 / 3rd $0.35 / 4th $0.25 / 5th $0.17).
Gate (kit RULES.txt): `/output/entry.txt`, UTF-8, **1–199 characters**,
not only whitespace, one line. Exact ties go to the smaller text
(fewer bytes), then the earlier receipt — so shorter is better.

## Judge brief criteria (in order — first difference decides)

1. A single-line joke about office life (not a story, slogan or list).
2. Turns on a real detail of office life (meetings, email, printer, coffee machine).
3. Lands its laugh in the last few words.
4. Clear on one reading, no setup that needs explaining.
5. Fresh wording, not a joke already going round the internet.

Each candidate is scored 1–5 per criterion, total /25.
Penalties applied honestly: the "meeting could have been an email" trope
(#15) and the "remove me from this thread" trope (#2) lose on criterion 5
because those exact jokes circulate widely; "PC LOAD LETTER" (#13) loses
on criterion 4 because it needs the reader to know the error code.
